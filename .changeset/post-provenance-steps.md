Redesign the provenance card on published posts so readers can tell what is done and what is still in progress. The heading says whether the story is permanently stored or also timestamped on Bitcoin, and two steps show the Arweave storage and the Bitcoin timestamp with their date and block. A pending Bitcoin timestamp shows as in progress instead of a raw "pending confirmation" label, and the Arweave step no longer reads as pending while the story is already stored. The transaction IDs, the SHA-256 of the stored story and the `.ots` proof move to a collapsed "Verify it yourself" section with copy buttons and a link to check the proof on opentimestamps.org.
