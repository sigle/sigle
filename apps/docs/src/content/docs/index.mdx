Sigle is a secured and open-source writing platform for web3 content creators, crypto analysts and more.

## How it works

On Sigle, published posts are stored permanently on Arweave and signed with the author's Stacks wallet, so anyone can verify who wrote them. Posts are also notarized on Bitcoin with OpenTimestamps, giving writers cryptographic proof of authorship.

## Testnet

Sigle is currently in a public testnet, anyone can create posts and use the SDK and API to integrate or interact with the protocol. To interact with the protocol, you will need a Stacks wallet to sign in and sign your posts. Publishing is free and doesn't require testnet tokens.

You can find the testnet at [testnet.sigle.io](https://testnet.sigle.io).
